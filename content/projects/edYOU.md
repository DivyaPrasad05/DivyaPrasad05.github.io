This project was submitted to HackMIT 2024.

We build a webapp that used LLMs to accelerate learning rate for students. We had 3 main features:
1) Video Resources: It brings in videos related to the topic searched up
2) Image Resources: Our AI creates custom made images related to the study topic, often incorporating diagrams
3) Audio Resources: Using the Suno API and its powerful AI features, custom made songs were created for the search topic

It was my first international hackathon that I've ever been too (in fact, my first hackathon outside of Alberta haha). Was lucky enough to collaborate with the best and incredibly diverse team - one guy was even from Singapore! There were so many times during this whole experience where my my mind was blown 🤯 looking at different projects, learning about new technologies, and meeting super cool people. 

This whole experience was one taken out of my wildest dreams and I would love to come again for a round 2!

Check out our [[Github repo]](https://github.com/gs0930/EDYOU)
