This was my intern project that I completed as part of the [WISEST Summer Research Program](https://www.ualberta.ca/en/women-in-scholarship-engineering-science-technology/programs/summer-research-program/index.html).

Using vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, I created a website interface to store predictive text models developed for Plains Cree users.

This project was actually what got me interested in coding and the computer science field as a whole and what got me to pursue CS in university. Looking back, this project and program changed the trajectory of my life
